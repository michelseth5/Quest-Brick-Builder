## LDraw parts library

Brick shapes are generated from the [LDraw](https://www.ldraw.org) parts library,
used under the [Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 license (CC BY 2.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/).
Thanks to the LDraw.org community and part authors.

The library itself isn't stored in this repository; download `complete.zip` from
https://library.ldraw.org and unzip it into an `LDraw` folder next to `Assets`.
